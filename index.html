Quick answers

FAQ

How do I calculate 20% of 250?

Multiply 250 by 0.20. The result is 50.

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100.

Can I calculate a sale price?

Yes. Choose Discount, enter the original price and discount percentage, and the calculator shows the final price and savings.
